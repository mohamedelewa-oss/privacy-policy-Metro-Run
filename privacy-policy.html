Privacy policy

Metro Run is a game for children. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no chat, and it does not use the internet.

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no permission to use the internet, so nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

Game progress (levels, coins, outfits, settings and the hero name you type) is saved only on this phone. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this progress in the phone's own backup in the owner's Google account. We cannot see or open that backup.

The listen buttons use the voice that is already installed on the phone to say a word. Only the word itself is passed to that voice.

In the parent area, the Share button lets a grown-up send the save code with an app they choose. This only happens when they tap it.

The parent area is protected by a question for grown-ups.
